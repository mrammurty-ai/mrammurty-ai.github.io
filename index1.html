  • Math 491/812: (Fall Semester) Introduction to p-adic analysis
    Text: Instructor's notes.

    This is an introduction to p-adic analysis and is cross-listed as a graduate course. The first half of the course will be very elementary and begin with the study of p-adic metrics. The last half will be devoted to applications to number theory and the study of p-adic zeta functions. We will meet in Room 422, Jeffery Hall, every tuesday from 3-5pm.

    Suggested reference: N. Koblitz, p-adic numbers, p-adic analysis and zeta functions, Springer-Verlag.
